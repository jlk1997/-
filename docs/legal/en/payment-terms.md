# BitYin Payment Terms

**Effective date: 20 July 2026**  
**Version: 2026-07-20**

## 1. What you buy

BitYin sells credit packs and monthly membership. Credits are consumed by AI music generation and similar features. Members get extra perks such as monthly credits and priority queue.

Listed pack and plan prices are in CNY. Stripe charges your card in the currency your card network settles; we do not invent a separate USD catalog.

## 2. Virtual goods

Credits are virtual goods/services. **Once purchase succeeds, credits are added to your balance.** **No refunds** except where law or these terms require one.

## 3. Price and payment

1. The price on the purchase page at checkout is the price
2. Channels: WeChat Pay (mini program / China H5), Alipay, Stripe (cards on the browser app)
3. Credits land when payment completes

Credit tips and royalty fiat never mix. Radio royalties settle only after a certified listen check-in.

## 4. Subscriptions and renewal

1. Monthly membership may try to renew if you turned on auto-renew
2. Manage or cancel auto-renew in the payment channel (WeChat / Alipay / Stripe)
3. After cancel, remaining time in the current period still applies
4. China WeChat/Alipay plans may require manual renewal until native recurring billing is available

## 5. Credit rules

1. Credits are not transferable and not cash
2. Unused credits are void after account deletion
3. We may change credit costs with prior notice

## 6. Invoices

China e-invoices: send order details to privacy@vibe-sorcery.com. We aim to process within 15 business days. Overseas card purchases do not use China fapiao.

## 7. Errors

If a system fault double-charges you, contact support for a verified refund.
