# BitYin Community Guidelines

**Effective date: 20 July 2026**  
**Version: 2026-07-20**

Please follow these rules so the community stays usable for creators.

## 1. Encouraged

- Original or licensed remix music
- Creative sharing
- Constructive comments
- Comments, nicknames, and bios are screened for banned terms; violations may be blocked or filtered
- Official challenges and community events

## 2. Prohibited

1. Content that breaks the law
2. Sexual, violent, terror, or gambling content
3. Infringement of copyright, trademark, or publicity rights
4. False information, rumors, or defamation
5. Spam, bait-and-share, fake engagement
6. Unlawful AI deep-synthesis content

## 3. Remix licenses

When you publish, you choose a remix license:

- **Allow remix**: others may create derivatives
- **Attribution**: remixes must credit you
- **No remix**: derivatives are not allowed

The choice takes effect when you publish.

## 4. Reports

1. Use Report on the work menu
2. We review reports as soon as we can
3. Outcomes may include takedown, warning, feature limits, or a permanent ban

## 5. Consequences

Repeat or serious violations can lead to a ban. Unused credits are not refunded except where the law requires it.

## 6. Contact

- Reports: privacy@vibe-sorcery.com
