# BitYin Minor Protection

**Effective date: 20 July 2026**  
**Version: 2026-07-20**

炼金音坊 protects minors’ personal information under the Law of the People’s Republic of China on the Protection of Minors and the Personal Information Protection Law.

## 1. Age

1. The service is intended for users **aged 14 and over**
2. Users **under 14** may not register on their own
3. Users under 14 may use a guardian’s account only with **the guardian’s consent and presence**

## 2. Guardians

1. Guide reasonable use and screen time
2. Supervise community posts
3. Delete the account in Settings to remove a minor’s information

## 3. Payments

1. Paid features should be used only with a guardian’s consent and supervision
2. If a minor spends a large amount without consent, the guardian may contact support for review

## 4. Data

1. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 14
2. If we find we collected it by mistake, we will delete it
