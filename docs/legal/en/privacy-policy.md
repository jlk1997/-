# BitYin Privacy Policy

**Effective date: 20 July 2026**  
**Version: 2026-07-20**

BitYin (BitTune, “we”, “us”, or “the service”) is operated by 炼金音坊. The browser app is the full product. The WeChat mini program is an optional China login and virtual-pay channel — you do not need it to use BitYin. We protect personal information under the Personal Information Protection Law of the People’s Republic of China and other applicable rules. **Please read this policy before using the service. We do not collect your personal information until you accept this policy.**

## 1. Information we collect

| Type | Examples | How collected | Purpose |
|------|----------|---------------|---------|
| Account | Email, username, hashed password, display name, avatar, bio | You provide it | Register, sign in, identify you |
| WeChat | OpenID; nickname and avatar only if you authorize | WeChat login (mini program only) | Mini-program login, account bind, virtual pay |
| Creative input | Uploaded audio, text intent, lyrics, generated works | You provide it | AI music generation, mood analysis |
| Activity | Browse, play, like, comment, collect | Automatic | Recommendations, product improvement |
| Device | Device model, OS, IP address | Automatic | Security, stability |
| Transactions | Order IDs, payment amounts, credit ledger | During payment | Billing, reconciliation, support |
| Connector sync | Plays, tracks, and regions you check on a dashboard you already logged into | You tap Sync to BitTune on the official page | Creator desk display and revenue estimates; settlement only after a second grant |
| Logs | Access time, actions, error logs | Automatic | Debugging, security audit |

WeChat fields are collected only if you sign in with WeChat or use the mini program.

## 2. WeChat mini program (China channel only)

If you use the BitYin WeChat mini program:

1. **When we collect**: only after you accept the Terms of Service and this Privacy Policy and confirm WeChat’s privacy authorization prompt.
2. **WeChat OpenID**: identifies your account, keeps you signed in, and completes mini-program virtual pay via `wx.login` after authorization.
3. **Nickname and avatar (optional)**: for your profile; you may refuse. Basic browsing still works; some social features may be limited.
4. **Storage**: account and order data are stored on servers in the People’s Republic of China. Passwords are stored with irreversible hashing.
5. We do **not** sell your personal information to unrelated third parties.

The browser product does not require WeChat authorization.

## 3. How we use information

1. Provide, maintain, and improve BitYin (AI music, community, playback)
2. Identity verification, account security, and risk control
3. Credit purchases, subscriptions, and after-sales support
4. Personalized recommendations only with your consent
5. Legal compliance and cooperation with regulators when required

## 5. Storage and security

- **Location**: People’s Republic of China
- **Retention**: while the account exists, plus any legally required period after deletion; creative content follows your settings
- **Safeguards**: HTTPS in transit, password hashing, access control, periodic security review

4. **Withdraw consent** (turn off personalized recommendations, or delete the account)
5. **Delete the account** (Settings → delete account; 30-day cooling-off, then permanent deletion)

Email privacy@vibe-sorcery.com to exercise these rights.
